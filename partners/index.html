Membership is separate. Recruiting another member never creates a commission, an upline, or a claim on their earnings.

01 / Work we can pay for

Useful work has a home.

Customer referrals

Introduce a real business that becomes a paying Forkalope customer under published attribution terms.

Migrations and onboarding

Move repositories, teams, automation, and people onto a working deployment.

Support and training

Help a customer’s team learn the workflow and keep their deployment healthy.

Infrastructure operation

Run approved nodes, participate in recovery drills, and deliver the responsibilities in an operator agreement.

02 / Published rules

No downlines.
No founder multipliers.

Everyone starts at zero. The same opportunity applies whether you join tomorrow or five years from now. Rates, eligibility, attribution, tax documents, payment timing, and any recurring commission duration will be published before a program becomes active.

This page describes a planned program. It is not an offer of income, a guarantee of work, or an investment opportunity.
